How a week comes together

Tell Yummzi who you cook for, what you can spend and how much time you have. It does the rest.

  1. Pick your store

    Choose from more than 28,000 US supermarkets, or stores in Canada and the UK. Prices follow the store you pick.

  2. Get a week that fits

    Twenty-one meals from more than 300 recipes, around your budget, diet and time. Swap any meal and see what it does to the bill.

  3. Shop one list

    Everything the week needs, sorted by aisle with a price on each item. Share it so anyone at home can tick things off.

  4. Cook it step by step

    Cook mode gives amounts for your table, built-in timers and what to look for at each step.

Riggy trims the bill
