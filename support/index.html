Support

Have a question, found a problem, or want to delete your data? Send us a message and we will reply by email, usually within two working days.

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Yummzi and tap Cancel Subscription. To cancel during the 3-day free trial without being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought a membership but it isn't showing.

In the app, open Settings › Yummzi membership and tap Restore, signed in with the same Apple Account you paid with.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings › Delete account. Your account and everything synced to it are deleted. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Are the prices exact?

No. Grocery prices are estimates based on your store and region, so the total at the till can differ.
