Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

This policy explains what the Yummzi app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. Yummzi is made by Mahmoud Aidaros, an independent developer based in Egypt ("we", "us"). If you have a question, write to us through the support page.

The short version

  • Yummzi works without an account. Your meal plans stay on your phone unless you sign in.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can delete your account and everything synced to it from inside the app, at any time.

What we collect, and why

When you use the app without signing in

Your household details, meal plans, grocery list and preferences are stored on your device only. Basic usage events (for example "opened the grocery list") are also kept only on your device and are never sent to us.

Account, if you sign in

If you choose Sign in with Apple, we receive an account identifier from Apple and, if you allow it, your name and email address (which may be a private relay address from Apple). We use them only to give you an account so your kitchen can be backed up and moved to a new phone.

Your kitchen, if you sign in

When you are signed in, we store a copy of your kitchen so it can be backed up and synced: your household size and the details you choose to enter (such as names, ages, diets, allergies and foods to avoid), your budget, your chosen store and ZIP or postal code, your weekly plans, grocery list, pantry items, recipe ratings and saved recipes, your notes on recipes, prices you enter yourself, reminder settings, the takeout price you entered, and the notes Riggy keeps about how your household eats (you can see and delete these in Settings). We use this data only to run these features for you.

Shared grocery lists

If you share a grocery list, the people you invite can see and edit its items and the names you give the list and its members. To join a list without an account, the app creates an anonymous session for that person.

Riggy, the kitchen helper

When you message Riggy, your message and a snapshot of your current week (meals, budget, household preferences and grocery estimate) are sent to our Riggy server so it can answer and suggest changes. To write its replies, Riggy uses an AI model provided through OpenRouter and DeepSeek, which receive the conversation for that purpose. Conversations are kept on our Riggy server so a conversation can continue; ask us through the support page and we will delete them. Riggy changes nothing in your plan until you tap Apply.

Location

If you tap "Use my location" when choosing a store, the app reads your approximate location once and uses it on your device to find nearby stores. Your location is not sent to us. The ZIP or postal code that results is saved with your store choice.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm your membership: it receives your purchase receipts and an app user identifier, not your payment details.

Suggestions and support messages

If you send us a suggestion in the app or a message through the support page, we store what you write (and the email address you give us, if any) so we can read it and reply.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and server functions (in the European Union).
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and handles payments.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription status.
  • OpenRouter and DeepSeek provide the AI model Riggy uses to reply.
  • A cloud hosting provider runs the Riggy server.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us. We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

We keep your account data for as long as you have an account. To delete it, open Settings › Delete account in the app: your account and everything synced to it are deleted from our database. Data on your phone stays until you delete the app. Subscription records kept by Apple and RevenueCat follow their own retention rules. To delete Riggy conversations or support messages, or to ask for a copy of your data, contact us through the support page.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict how we use it. Contact us through the support page and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Yummzi is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each account can only read its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page, and tell you in the app when the change is significant.
